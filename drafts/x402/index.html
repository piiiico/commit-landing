Buyers who come back

For 38 sellers (the 20 largest by 7-day volume plus the 20 with the most 7-day buyers): how many distinct buyer wallets paid them in at least 2 of the last 4 weeks. A buyer who comes back is a choice made with money twice. Two kinds of flow are removed before counting. First, wallets that also receive x402 payments (buyer wallet is among the top 1000 receivers (30d)), because a ring of wallets paying each other looks like demand. Second, any single payment of $100 or more, because that is a transfer, not a service call. A wallet is not a person: one operator can run many wallets, and nothing here can tell them apart. Read the count as returning wallets, not returning customers.

Method
